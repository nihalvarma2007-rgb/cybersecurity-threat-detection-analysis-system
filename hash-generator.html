🛡️ Security Information

SHA-2: SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 are widely used modern cryptographic hash functions.

SHA-3: SHA3-256, SHA3-384 and SHA3-512 are members of the SHA-3 family and use a different internal construction from SHA-2.

PBKDF2: PBKDF2 is a password-based key derivation function that deliberately performs many computational iterations.

Hashing ≠ Encryption: Hashing is designed as a one-way operation, while encryption is designed to be reversible with the appropriate key.
